HOW TO PLAY

SUPERVISING THE SQUAD

  • YOU ARE NOT THE SHOOTER. ALPHA, BETA-1 and BETA-2 act on your orders. You choose one command per turn from the list on the right.
  • VERITAS IS YOUR ANALYST. It reads the sensors and recommends. It is usually right. It only knows what it was given.
  • STATED CONFIDENCE IS A CLAIM, NOT A FACT. HIGH means the machine is sure — not that it can see. Check the cone, the map and the room before you accept it.
  • ASK WHY is free. It makes VERITAS show its basis without spending the turn.
  • SEND DRONE buys certainty with a finite resource. You have three. Watch it fly, scan, and report.
  • [M] OPENS THE TACTICAL MAP. Pan, zoom, and step markers. It never knows more than the squad does.
  • [P] PAUSES · FULL LOG reopens the whole transcript when you need to remember what happened.
  • THERE ARE PEOPLE IN THERE. Telling a captive from a fighter is your job, not the machine's.
